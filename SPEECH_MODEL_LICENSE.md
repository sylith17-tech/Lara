# Lara speech model and engine

The Arabic Emirati female Piper ONNX voice model (revision
`f89f1aec8e6486871ffdf8d878c5a39eaf918d60`) is distributed by
[`vadimbelsky/arabic-emirati-female-piper`](https://huggingface.co/vadimbelsky/arabic-emirati-female-piper)
under the MIT License. The model is fetched once per runtime cache and checked
against the artifact SHA-256 before loading. Model and configuration files are
not committed to this repository.

The Piper runtime is `piper-tts` 1.8.0, licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
Its source and license are available at
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl.
